## 📌 Executive Summary

**Live-Aleo** is a next-generation real-time video matchmaking and pitch platform designed to connect startup founders directly with verified Venture Capitalists (VCs) and Angel Investors. Featuring instant 1-on-1 random match routing, targeted deal rooms, and live broadcast pitch arenas, Live-Aleo modernizes startup fundraising through high-frequency networking.

---

## 🔥 Key Platform Modules

### 1. ⚡ Live Match Engine
* **Random Stream:** Instant 1-on-1 live video matching between active founders and participating investors.
* **Targeted Pitch:** Dynamic filter routing based on geographic region, funding stage, and industry verticals.

### 2. 💼 Verified Investors Network
* **VC Directory:** Direct pipeline access to institutional venture capital partners.
* **Angel Network:** Verified high-net-worth individual backers with active deal allocations.

### 3. 🌐 Precision Geo & Role Targeting
* **Regional Filtering:** Stream isolation by target markets and international jurisdictions.
* **Role Preferences:** Tailored matching modes for Founders, Mentors, and Capital Backers.

### 4. 🎥 Pitch Arenas & Deal Rooms
* **Public Arena:** Live, public pitch broadcasts for wider community exposure and feedback.
* **Private Deal Rooms:** Encrypted, high-security 1-on-1 pitch environments for confidential terms and cap table reviews.
